About Me

Hi, I'm Bree.

I'm currently pursuing a Bachelor of Science in Psychology at Washington State University while completing a minor in Digital Technology & Culture and a certificate in Science & Technology Writing. My work lives at the intersection of psychology, technology, design, and creative expression. Whether I'm building a website, conducting research, producing music, or exploring new AI workflows, I'm driven by one question: How can this idea be expressed more thoughtfully, more completely, and in a way that creates the greatest impact?

I'm at my best in self-directed environments where I can move fluidly between research, writing, design, music, coding, and emerging AI tools. Working independently allows me to connect ideas across disciplines and develop projects from concept to completion while maintaining a cohesive creative vision. I created neurocloudy as a place where all of those worlds could finally live together. This portfolio is a collection of that journey, bringing together academic research, technology, design, and creative work as I continue exploring how innovation can support human flourishing. Welcome to my little corner of the cloud.
